If cost per appointment doubles this week

How I would triage it, in the order I would check. Try it: lower the bookings by raising ad spend in the dashboard and see which targets break.

  1. Tracking first. Confirm deposits and bookings still reach Meta and match the calendar. A tracking break looks like a cost spike.
  2. Which layer moved. Compare CPM, click-through rate and landing page conversion against last week. Cost per appointment is the product of all three.
  3. Creative fatigue. Check frequency and the age of the top ads. If the winner is old, launch new hooks on the same offer.
  4. Booking page and calendar. Test the page on a phone. Confirm slots are open and the deposit step loads.
  5. Changes on our side. Review the account change log for edits to budget, audience or bidding in the last seven days.

On the client call: lead with the number, name the layer that moved, say what I have already changed, and give the date I expect it to recover.
